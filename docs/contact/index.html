Why am I seeing this page?

This URL is a technical shortcut (a placeholder) used by Birthday Adapter to link your calendar events directly back to your Android contacts.

Because Android didn't redirect this link into the app, it means your system is currently forcing these links to open in the browser instead.

How to fix this in Android

You can easily restore the direct link functionality by changing your Android system settings:

  1. Open your phone's Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps → All Apps (or App Management).
  3. Find and select Birthday Adapter from the list.
  4. Scroll down and tap on Open by default (or Supported Links).
  5. Make sure the toggle for Open supported links is turned ON.
  6. Ensure that birthdayadapter.heinisch.fr is checked or listed as a supported domain.

Once enabled, tapping this link in your calendar will immediately open the correct contact card inside your contacts app instead of loading this website.
