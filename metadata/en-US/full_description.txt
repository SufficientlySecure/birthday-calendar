<b>Description</b>
Birthday Adapter provides birthdays, anniversaries, and other events from your contacts directly into
your Android calendar. Unlike other apps, it integrates seamlessly with your system calendar, so you
can see all your important dates alongside your other events.

<b>Features</b>
• Complete integration of birthdays, anniversaries, and other events from Android's contacts to the Android calendar system
• Filter contact events based on account and contact groups
• Event Reminders
• Jubilee markers
• Customize all event titles
• Chose which events will have reminders
• Color picker for the birthday calendar
